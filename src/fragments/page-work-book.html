​Get Dreams Done on Purpose: Working Out the Meaning in Your Mission

It is a transformative workbook by Mike Kelly, designed to help leaders and teams bridge the gap between purpose and performance. Building upon the principles from his previous work, Meaning Is the Mission, this hands-on guide offers practical exercises and deep introspection to align your mission with actionable outcomes.

Why This Book Matters

Whether you’re a seasoned executive or an emerging leader, this workbook serves as a valuable resource to navigate the complexities of modern work environments and achieve goals with intentionality.

Personal and Professional Growth

Actionable Insights
