MEANING IS THE MISSION

Why are we spending more money than ever to make work better, only to see it get worse?

Billions of dollars and countless time is spent each year to improve the employee and customer experience, and yet each year, surveys show that we’re more miserable than ever. The effects of this misery crop up in all kinds of symptoms: the remote/hybrid workplace debate, rampant imposter syndrome, and fears of having our jobs replaced by AI.

With all this in mind, it begs the question: is the way we work working?

If you’re not sure, then this book is for you.

Mike Kelly is the founder and CEO of TeamOnUP, a company dedicated to recapturing value for companies by accelerating productivity and performance for each employee. With over 20 years in senior leadership roles within the credit union industry, Mike’s strategic and visionary approach has been instrumental in building organizations that achieve key outcomes. Mike’s reputation as a thought leader and subject matter expert in his field is solidified by his status as a sought-after keynote speaker. Mike lives in Franklin, Tennessee with his wife Jessica and their three children, Cashel, Maebyn, and Colm.
